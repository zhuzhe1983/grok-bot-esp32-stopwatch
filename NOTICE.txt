This standalone demo extracts the procedural interaction renderer from the local
esp-agent working tree at the user's request. Extraction hashes are recorded in
docs/extraction.json. No license grant for the original project is changed here.

Grok Bot is a visual reference. This project is not an official xAI product and
does not include extracted xAI animation assets or the original player.
